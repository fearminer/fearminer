# Security

Report a vulnerability in FearMiner privately, to security@fearminer.com,
rather than in a public issue: anything that could redirect or lose mining
rewards, run code on a rig through the miner or its stats endpoint, or make
the miner send something it does not say it sends (see "What the miner
talks to" in the README).

Include what you observed, how to reproduce it, the version (`fearminer
--version`) and the platform. You get an acknowledgement within five
business days and a fix in a new release; only the latest release is
supported. Research done in good faith, on your own rigs, without
disrupting pools or other people's machines, is welcome and will not be
pursued.
